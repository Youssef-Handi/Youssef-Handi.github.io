I am a statistician and postdoctoral researcher at **Western University**, Canada.
I hold a Ph.D. in Mathematics and Statistics from **Université du Québec à
Montréal (UQAM)**, where I worked under the supervision of Karim Oualkacha and
Mhamed Mesfioui, and an M.Sc. in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics from
**Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières (UQTR)**.

My research focuses on statistical methods for modelling complex dependence
structures and conducting inference for dependent and high-dimensional data.
I develop copula-based regression frameworks for multivariate binary outcomes
and mixed continuous–discrete data, with particular interests in robust and
asymmetric regression and quantile and expectile inference. My current work
extends these ideas to non-Gaussian spatial and spatio-temporal models,
high-dimensional testing, and stochastic processes, with applications in
environmental monitoring, reliability, and risk management.

My applied experience informs these methodological interests. At **Hydro-Québec’s
research institute (IREQ)**, I developed Bayesian methods for combining expert
opinions with equipment performance data and implemented MCMC-based prediction
tools for reliability analysis. As a data scientist at the **National Bank of
Canada**, I worked on machine-learning models for credit-risk classification,
stress testing, and data pipelines with quality controls.

## Research Interests

- Copulas, dependence modelling, and regression for binary and mixed data.
- Robust and asymmetric regression; quantile and expectile inference.
- High-dimensional inference and regression testing.
- Non-Gaussian spatial and spatio-temporal statistics.
- Bayesian methods, statistical learning, and stochastic processes.
