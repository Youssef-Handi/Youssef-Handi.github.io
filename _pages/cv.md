## Research Profile

I am a mathematical statistician and postdoctoral researcher at Western University,
working at the intersection of mathematical statistics, statistical learning,
copula-based dependence modelling, high-dimensional inference, spatial and
spatio-temporal statistics, and stochastic processes. My research develops rigorous,
interpretable, and computationally tractable methods for dependent,
high-dimensional, imbalanced, non-Gaussian, and spatially structured data, as well as
data involving rare events, with applications in health, reliability, environmental
monitoring, risk management, and data science.
